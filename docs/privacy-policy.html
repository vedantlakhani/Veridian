Veridian Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

Veridian collects the following information to provide the service:

  • Account information: Email address, display name, and profile photo.
  • Emission data: Carbon emission entries you log (food, transport, energy quantities).
  • Usage data: App usage patterns to improve the experience.
  • Push notification tokens: Device tokens for delivering reminders, if you grant permission.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your personalised carbon footprint dashboard.
  • To generate AI-powered insights via Anthropic's Claude API (processed server-side — your data is sent to Claude to generate insights, not stored by Anthropic beyond their standard API processing).
  • To enable social challenges and leaderboards (only reduction percentage is visible to other participants — never raw emission values).
  • To send daily logging reminders if you opt in.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security — only you can access your emission entries. Data is stored in the EU-West region.

4. Third Parties

  • Supabase: Database, authentication, and file storage provider. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic (Claude API): AI insights generated server-side via Edge Functions. Emission data is sent to the Claude API to generate personalised insights. Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Expo / EAS: App infrastructure and push notification delivery. Expo Privacy Policy

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained while your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@veridian.app

7. Children

Veridian is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We will notify users of material changes via the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions? Contact: privacy@veridian.app
