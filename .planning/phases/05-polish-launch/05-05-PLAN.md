# Veridian — Store Metadata

## App Store (iOS)

### App Name
Veridian: Carbon Tracker

### Subtitle (30 chars max)
Track & reduce your footprint

### Short Description (170 chars max)
Track your personal carbon footprint across food, transport, and energy. Get AI-powered insights and daily actions — powered by Claude. Challenge friends to reduce together.

(Character count: 170 — exact limit)

### Full Description (4000 chars max)
Veridian helps you understand your true carbon impact and take one meaningful step to reduce it — every single day.

**Track Every Emission**
Log food, transport, and energy emissions in seconds using DEFRA 2025 GHG conversion factors — the same data used by UK government reporting. See your daily, weekly, and monthly carbon totals on a beautiful dashboard.

**AI-Powered Insights**
Every day, Claude analyses your emission profile and gives you one specific, quantified action: "Switch to plant-based protein twice this week to save ~2.4 kg CO₂e." Not generic tips — personalised to what you actually logged.

**Challenge Friends**
Create or join reduction challenges with friends. Watch the leaderboard update in real time as everyone logs their entries. Earn achievement badges for streaks, reductions, and milestones.

**Built for Privacy**
Your raw emission data is never visible to other users — only your reduction percentage appears on leaderboards. AI insights are generated server-side; your data never touches a client-side API key.

**Works Offline**
Log entries without a connection. Veridian queues them locally and syncs when you reconnect — your streak is safe.

**Why Carbon Tracking Matters**
The average person emits ~8 tonnes of CO₂e per year. Veridian makes the invisible visible — because you can't reduce what you don't measure.
